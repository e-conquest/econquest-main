# Governance

EconQuest Terminal is an open-source research and teaching project. It is not a commercial
product and does not sell data, signals or rankings.

## Who decides

The project currently has one maintainer, who reviews and merges changes, sets the roadmap and
decides on releases. Decisions are made in public issues, pull requests and Discussions wherever
possible, with the reason written down.

| Role | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Maintainer | @smcarus |

## Rules that do not change by vote or by funding

These are the project's research rules. A change to one needs a public proposal and at least four
weeks for comment.

1. Only real figures: a number appears only when an official series, a live feed or the user's own
   file supplied it. Anything else reads Missing with the reason.
2. Tests are pre-registered and hashed before they run. A recorded verdict is never changed, and a
   failed model is not re-tuned until it passes.
3. Each data source is added with a row in the data licence register.
4. Donors and sponsors have no say over methods, verdicts, Quest outcomes, country coverage or
   wording.

## Becoming a maintainer

A contributor with a record of reviewed, merged work over at least three months can be invited by
the existing maintainers. Once there are three or more maintainers, decisions that cannot reach
agreement are settled by a simple majority of maintainers.

## Money

Until the project has a fiscal host, it takes no donations. Once it does, every gift and expense is
shown on the host's public ledger, spending follows a published budget, and a short report on money
in and out is posted every quarter.
